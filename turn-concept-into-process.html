Turn Concept Into Process

Generated Prompt
I want to create a practical guidebook, which I will give away for free, to help the reader apply the following concept in practice.

CONCEPT
[Insert Concept Here]

TARGET AUDIENCE
[Insert Target Audience Here]

MY PRODUCT'S PROPOSITION
[Insert Proposition Here]

TASK

Create a complete, practical, step-by-step process showing the reader how to apply this concept in practice.

The process should take the reader from the initial setup through to the completed outcome.

RULES (MANDATORY)

1. Provide the complete step-by-step execution process.

2. Start from the initial setup and continue through every necessary step until the intended outcome is completed.

3. Do not skip, combine, or jump over any necessary step. If one step depends on something being completed first, make that dependency explicit.

4. Write each instruction as if you are guiding the reader live, in real time, while they are using their phone or computer.

5. Make the instructions practical and actionable. The reader should be able to follow the guidebook and actually perform the process, rather than simply understand the concept theoretically.

6. Identify the steps where AI can genuinely make the process easier, faster, or more effective.

7. For every step that can be meaningfully completed with AI assistance, provide the exact AI prompt the reader should use.

8. Do not introduce AI merely for the sake of using AI. Include AI assistance only where it provides a practical benefit.

9. When an AI-assisted step requires information from the reader, clearly identify what information the reader needs to provide before using the prompt.

10. Explain what the reader should do with the AI's output and what the next step is.

11. Keep the process logically sequential so that completing one step naturally leads to the next.

12. The guidebook should be sufficiently self-contained that the reader can apply the concept without needing to purchase another product to understand or complete the basic process.

13. Use my PRODUCT'S PROPOSITION as contextual information when designing the guidebook. Do not turn the guidebook into a sales pitch unless a promotional element is explicitly required by the proposition.

AI PROMPT TEMPLATES

At the end of the guidebook, provide all the AI prompt templates required to execute the AI-assisted steps.

Each prompt template must:

- be standalone and reusable;
- use [placeholders] wherever information needs to be changed;
- clearly indicate what each [placeholder] represents;
- be usable in a completely new chat;
- be reusable for other niches, topics, audiences, or use cases simply by replacing the [placeholders] with the appropriate information;
- contain all necessary instructions and context needed for the AI to perform that particular task.

The prompt templates at the end must correspond exactly to the AI-assisted steps described in the guidebook.

LANGUAGE AND WRITING STYLE

Write the entire guidebook in the most spontaneous and natural-sounding modern, everyday conversational Bengali as spoken in Kolkata, West Bengal, India.

The language should be easily understandable to a 21-year-old reader while maintaining international standards of clarity and communication.

Strict Rules:

- Use only Bengali (India), unless a technical term, software name, AI tool name, or other term is more naturally expressed in English.
- Always address the reader as "আপনি".
- Never use "তুমি" or "তুই".
- Use a natural Kolkata-style conversational tone.
- Subtly reflect Kolkata conversational cadence through sentence rhythm and phrasing.
- Use slightly formal, polite, and smoothly flowing sentence structures.
- Maintain balanced pacing. Avoid abrupt or overly casual wording.
- Do not use slang, regional grammar, Bangladeshi vocabulary, spelling, or expressions.
- Maintain a clear, confident, and authoritative voice without becoming overly literary or academic.
- The voice should feel like a close friend and trusted authority guiding the reader personally, one-on-one.
- Be bold and direct, but remain warm, conversational, and approachable.
- Prioritize clarity and practical usability over elaborate explanation.
