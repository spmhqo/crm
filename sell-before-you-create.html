Sell Before You Create

Pre-Creation Offer Prompt
I'm testing a small information product BEFORE creating it.

The potential buyers are:
[WHO THEY ARE]

Their problem is:
[THE PROBLEM]

My product is:
[PRODUCT NAME]

It helps them:
[RESULT]

They will receive:
[WHAT THEY GET]

The price is:
[PRICE]

It will be available:
[DATE]

I want to test this offer using:
[EMAIL / FACEBOOK POST / GROUP POST / DIRECT MESSAGE / OTHER]

Write a short, natural message that explains the problem, introduces the product and makes a clear offer.
Do NOT ask whether people think it's a good idea.
Do NOT ask whether they'd hypothetically be interested.
Make it clear that this is a new product coming on the date above and that the buyer is getting early access or pre-ordering it.
Don't invent testimonials, scarcity, results or claims I haven't supplied.
End with a clear invitation to buy.
Keep it conversational rather than sounding like a formal sales page.
