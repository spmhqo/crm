Build My 5-Email Follow-Up
Here is my free report:
[my free report]

Here is my paid product:
[my paid product]

Create a five-email follow-up sequence.

Use this basic structure:
• Email 1: Deliver the report and tell them where to start
• Email 2: Expand one useful idea
• Email 3: Discuss a common mistake or difficulty
• Email 4: Introduce the bigger problem
• Email 5: Introduce the paid product

Continue the conversation started by the report.

Do not simply repeat the report or turn every email into a sales pitch.
