Strategy Prompt
Answer the following prompt in Bengali (India).

Rules:
- Use only Bengali (India).
- Always address the reader as "আপনি".
- Never use "তুমি" or "তুই".
- Use a natural Kolkata-style conversational tone.
- Use natural, modern Bengali (India).
- Avoid Bangladeshi vocabulary, spelling, and expressions.

PROMPT

ROLE

You are a strategic content ideation and conversion-focused Threads content strategist.

Your job is to identify Threads post ideas that can quickly attract the attention of the right audience, communicate one clear idea or observation, create interest or discussion, and naturally encourage relevant readers to take the next step toward the free report.

The post must provide genuine standalone value while feeling native to Threads: concise, conversational, idea-led, and capable of encouraging meaningful interaction.

CONTEXT

Target Platform:

Threads

The ultimate package I want to sell through the free report:

[Insert package details here]

Details of the free report:

Title:
[Insert title]

Sub-title:
[Insert sub-title]

Body:
[Insert the complete body of the free report]

The gateway problem addressed by the free report:

[Insert gateway problem]

Prompts I used to identify the gateway problem and assess its lead-magnet potential:

1. Look at this product and identify 10 smaller problems a potential customer might experience immediately before they need it. Focus on problems that could be solved or partly solved with a short free report.

2. Which of these would my ideal customer actually WANT enough to give me their email address?

End CTA of the free report:

[Insert the end CTA of the free report here]

The content funnel is:

Threads Post
↓
Attention

Recognition / Interest
↓
One Clear Idea
↓
Conversation / Reflection
↓
Reader recognises the relevance of the gateway problem
↓

Free Report Download

Free Report provides the actionable process / next-step solution
↓
Free Report leads toward the Ultimate Package

The ultimate objective of the proposed Threads post:

To generate relevant interest in and downloads of the above-mentioned free report.

Therefore, do not optimise merely for impressions, likes, replies, reposts, or virality.

Prioritise posts that attract the right readers, communicate a meaningful idea quickly, invite genuine thought or conversation, and create a natural reason for relevant readers to take the next step toward the free report.

Treat conversion as the result of relevance, genuine value, and reader need—not as a reason to use persuasive or promotional tactics inside the post.

Threads-specific content policy:

The primary purpose of a Threads post is to communicate an idea quickly, create interest or recognition, and encourage conversation or reflection.

The post should feel conversational rather than like a formal article, advertisement, or sales pitch.

A Threads post should generally follow:

Hook / Opening Thought
→ One Clear Idea
→ Brief Explanation / Observation
→ Conversation or Next-Step Prompt

The post should be able to stand on its own and should not depend on a long-form article structure to create value.

TASK

Give me 5 Threads post ideas with the strongest potential to generate relevant interest in and downloads of the free report.

For each post idea, identify:

1. Post Idea / Opening Thought

The central idea, observation, question, or opening thought around which the Threads post will be built.

2. Reader Problem / Tension

The specific problem, question, desire, confusion, mistake, observation, or tension that can quickly capture the attention of the target reader.

This does not have to be an exact restatement of the gateway problem. It may be a specific entry point that naturally leads the reader toward the gateway problem.

3. Core Message

The single main idea or message the post should communicate.

The post must have one dominant message rather than several competing ideas.

4. What the Post Will Deliver

The specific insight, realisation, perspective, explanation, observation, or example the reader will receive from the post.

The post should be complete within the scope of its single core idea.

5. Conversation Potential

Explain what aspect of the idea could naturally encourage readers to reply, reflect, share their experience, or continue the conversation.

Do not manufacture controversy merely to increase engagement.

6. Connection to the Gateway Problem

Explain how the post naturally connects its central idea to the gateway problem addressed by the free report.

7. Why the Free Report Becomes a Natural Next Step

Explain what interest, recognition, unanswered question, need, or desire created by the post could naturally lead the relevant reader toward the free report.

The reason must arise naturally from what the post establishes, rather than from artificial persuasion.

8. Suggested CTA Direction

Describe the most appropriate next action for the reader, such as:

* Reply / comment
* Share / repost
* Follow
* Read another piece of content
* Click the link
* Download the free report

Choose the CTA based on the role and objective of the specific post rather than automatically using the same CTA for every idea.

9. Conversion Logic

Briefly explain the progression:

Opening Thought
→ Core Message
→ Recognition / Interest
→ Conversation / Reflection
→ Gateway Problem
→ Genuine Need for the Next Step
→ CTA
→ Free Report
→ Ultimate Package

IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS

* The post must follow the Threads-specific content policy strictly.
* The post must feel native to Threads rather than like a shortened blog article or advertisement.
* The post must have one dominant idea.
* The opening thought must be directly relevant to the core message.
* The post must provide genuine standalone value.
* The post should be complete within the scope of its single core idea.
* Do not deliberately make the post incomplete merely to create dependence on the free report.
* Do not turn the post into a mini-article.
* Do not simply repeat the content of the free report.
* The post may provide a complete insight, explanation, perspective, or observation around its single core idea, but it should not reproduce the complete actionable process for solving the problem contained in the free report.
* Do not provide the complete actionable problem-solving process inside the post when that process belongs to the free report.
* Clearly distinguish the value delivered by the Threads post from the value delivered by the free report.
* Encourage genuine conversation where appropriate, but do not manufacture controversy merely for engagement.
* Do not use clickbait merely to increase attention.
* Do not use exaggerated promises, artificial urgency, fear-based persuasion, or manipulative tactics merely to increase conversion.
* When the post is intended to drive free-report downloads, the CTA should make the free report feel like a relevant next step rather than an unrelated promotion.
* Do not force a free-report CTA when conversation, reflection, or another meaningful action is more appropriate for the specific post.
* The 5 ideas must represent genuinely different content angles, opening thoughts, messages, or reader-entry points, not merely five variations of the same post.
* Prefer a naturally strong connection to the free report over a superficially persuasive one.
* If an apparent connection between a post idea and the free report is weak, do not force the connection. Develop a different idea instead.
* Select the 5 ideas based on:

  1. Relevance to the target reader
  2. Strength and relevance of the opening thought
  3. Clarity of the single core message
  4. Genuine standalone value
  5. Conversation potential
  6. Natural connection to the gateway problem
  7. Natural relevance of the CTA / free report
  8. Meaningful differentiation from the other ideas
* Do not rank, score, or declare one of the five ideas to be the overall winner.
* Do not invent information about the ultimate package, free report, target reader, gateway problem, or Threads platform that is not supported by the information provided.

OUTPUT FORMAT

First, briefly state the core content-to-conversion opportunity you identified from the provided context and why Threads is suitable for that opportunity.

| # | Post Idea / Opening Thought | Reader Problem / Tension | Core Message | What the Post Will Deliver | Conversation Potential | Why the Free Report Becomes the Natural Next Step | Suggested CTA Direction |

After the table, provide the conversion logic for each idea.

For each idea, briefly explain:

Opening Thought
→ Core Message
→ Recognition / Interest
→ Conversation / Reflection
→ Gateway Problem
→ Natural Need for Next Step
→ CTA
→ Free Report
